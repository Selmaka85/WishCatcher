# .ai_ram — ASTRA Genesis / WishCatcher

## CANONICAL PRODUCT MISSION — WISHCATCHER / ASTRA GENESIS (2026-11-04)

**Owner requirement / authoritative development objective.** Preserve this mission across
sessions and tasks; do not let the project collapse into a generic sentiment classifier,
chatbot, advertising detector, keyword monitor, or a purely academic LLM benchmark.

### What the product must ultimately discover
1. **Human wishes and unmet needs** — explicit requests AND implicit/contextual
   desires reconstructed from the correct speaker's multi-message discussion.
2. **Business opportunities** — recurring valuable problems, underserved segments,
   friction, costly workarounds, and possible services/products that solve them.
3. **Missing products or capabilities** — potentially novel products, absent features,
   desirable combinations of existing capabilities, and improvement opportunities.
   A user's inability to find a product is NOT proof that the product does not exist.
4. **Latent opportunities not suggested verbatim** — reason across independent,
   corroborated observations to propose testable solution hypotheses via Genesis,
   without fabricating customer statements or empirical market evidence.

### Causal product flow — the circuit to close
Allowed public-source collection -> preserve actor/thread/time and full context ->
contextual intent interpretation (optional local/opt-in LLM + conservative rules;
distinguish fact, inference, negation, supersession, resolved issues) ->
source-grounded need representation -> independent-actor/thread clustering ->
product/software/service gap investigation + competitor/availability checks ->
market/business feasibility research -> evidence/uncertainty-aware ranking ->
ASTRA Genesis logical component combination and alternative structures ->
reviewable opportunity/solution output, potentially a constrained MVP plan.

The **LLM is a semantic interpreter** extracting the discussion's main idea,
problem, desired outcome, failed alternatives, constraints and evidence; it is
NOT the single source of truth or an autonomous market validator. Rules,
provenance, contradiction gates and independent external checks are complementary.
Provider calls are opt-in; no surprise paid inference or unauthorized scraping.

### Minimum useful output contract (target, not current implementation claim)
For each opportunity report: source links and actor-specific evidence IDs;
explicit vs inferred need; target user/context; problem and desired outcome;
constraints/workarounds/rejected solutions; independent corroboration and
sample bias; proposed product/service or capability delta; comparable existing
alternatives and whether absence was actually checked; uncertainties; feasible
solution variants and Genesis component dependencies; reasons to prioritize
or reject; validation experiments; observed outcome and status.
Mark **product nonexistence, market size, willingness-to-pay, sales/revenue,
and execution feasibility as UNKNOWN** unless directly verified.

### Non-negotiable development / circle-closure gates
- **P1, interpretation:** benchmark with frozen, independently human-labeled
  natural RO/EN actor-specific conversations, including missing/reversed intent,
  hypothetical, third-party, informational and already-resolved cases. Report
  precision, recall, attribution, grounding, abstention and cost. Natural
  unlabeled Rules-vs-Qwen disagreements are investigation leads, not gold labels.
- **P2, discovery:** broaden lawful sources and independent search strata;
  measure source yield and coverage separately from classification quality.
  Never silently promote search snippets to original discussion evidence.
- **P3, evolution/persistence:** handle new replies, revisions, superseded goals,
  negative cache, cluster reconciliation, expiry and rollback. SQLite schema
  changes require separate impact review/owner approval.
- **P4, opportunity -> Genesis:** demonstrate end-to-end traces from actual
  conversation evidence to verified product/service gap hypotheses and
  domain-aware, dependency-consistent, compared solution structures.
  Test at least software, physical-product and service scenarios; a generic
  business-plan paragraph is not sufficient. External novelty, demand and
  practicality require independent validation, not LLM self-certification.
- **Before DONE:** show reproduced input -> actor-grounded inference ->
  independent corroboration -> realistic competitor/product check ->
  appropriately qualified business hypothesis -> Genesis composition ->
  tests on upstream/downstream consumers and documented residual gaps.
  Keep honest statuses: IMPLEMENTED, ENGINEERING-VERIFIED, EMPIRICALLY-VALIDATED,
  and OPEN are distinct.

### Operating rule for all future WishCatcher development
At the start of each task identify the affected node/contract and its contribution
to this mission; investigate root causes rather than adding duplicate detectors.
After each patch, run relevant tests and inspect downstream effects, update this
.ai_ram checkpoint and the architecture map when contracts change. Do NOT claim
mission completion from CI-green, a plausible LLM narrative, count of extracted
wishes, or unvalidated "new product" claims. Preserve current work branch and
never merge/release/migrate without owner authorization.

**Priority:** the end-to-end business-opportunity/product-gap discovery mission
is the north star; contextual LLM extraction and P1/P2/P3/P4 are means to that
outcome, not replacements for it. WishCatcher/Genesis may surface valuable
business ideas without obligating the owner to monetize this software itself.
